# Evidence validation after the pivot

The current product is [Feature Coordinator](mvp-brief.md), selected November 3, 2026. The former PTO experiment is preserved in [the archive](archive/feature-memory/evidence-experiment.md) and [experiment/](../experiment/README.md). It was never run against a provider and does not gate starting the new application foundation.

Validate the new workflow in the application as capabilities are built:

1. Explicit unanswered Confluence question: extract it, identify the configured business owner, and preserve source attribution.
2. Jira/Confluence/Figma mismatch: show the competing statements without inventing a resolution or assuming the newest is approved.
3. Benign design edit: avoid creating a business blocker or notifying every role for cosmetic changes.
4. PO reply: correlate it to the right question, propose the decision, and require the authorized approval before publishing.
5. Reliability: repeated refresh, duplicate events, delivery failure, missing reply, stale page version, human correction, and embedded instruction text.

Write expected findings before model calls, retain raw outputs and actual model/settings, and keep factual review separate from structural validation. Unknown usage/cost stays unknown. Do not turn an integration fixture into a claim of successful real delivery or publication.
